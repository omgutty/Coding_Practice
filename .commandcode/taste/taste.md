- For coding-interview practice questions, gives multiple solution options per problem rather than wanting a single best answer — expects the "obvious" solution to be shown but also wants alternatives (different techniques like filter/indexOf, reduce, Set, loops) with their trade-offs and complexity notes, and will keep asking for "other options" to collect more variants. Confidence: 0.5
